| Our Journey

The Elpaso Story

From a grassroots car wash to a full-service cleaning company — built on community trust.

2020 — The Beginning: Ishy's Car Wash & Sneaker Laundry

It all started as a grassroots, informal endeavour. Ishmael Keogatile began offering car wash and sneaker laundry services in the community, building trust and gaining invaluable hands-on experience along the way. The positive response from neighbours and locals proved there was a real need for quality, accessible cleaning services.

2023 — Formalising the Vision: Elpaso is Born

In October 2023, driven by a burning desire to revolutionise the cleaning industry, Ishmael laid the cornerstone of Elpaso Cleaning Solutions & Services. Recognising the barriers that confined quality services to affluent areas, the mission was crystal clear — break those barriers and make convenience and excellence accessible to all.

2024 — Expanding the Offering

Elpaso rapidly expanded beyond car washes and laundry to offer a full suite of services: industrial cleaning, commercial cleaning, residential deep cleans, denim and footwear care, landscaping, furniture removals, and event rentals. A true one-stop cleaning and services solution for the community.

Today — Growing With Purpose

Elpaso continues to grow, building meaningful community partnerships, supporting local initiatives, and investing in sustainable practices. Every doorstep served is a testament to the positive change Elpaso brings. The vision remains — to be the premier provider of comprehensive cleaning solutions in North West and beyond.

| Company Strategy

Mission, Vision & Goals

Our Mission

We are dedicated to delivering exceptional and holistic cleaning services that redefine cleanliness standards. Through a blend of fixed and mobile solutions, we aim to create a cleaner, safer, and more vibrant environment for individuals, households, and businesses.

Our Vision

To be the premier provider of comprehensive cleaning solutions — setting the standard for excellence and innovation, and making a lasting positive impact on our community. We envision a Rustenburg where every space, from the smallest home to the largest factory, shines with pride.

Our Objectives

To ensure customer satisfaction through exceptional, customer-focused services. We aim to lead through innovation, continuously embracing the latest industry trends. We are committed to creating a positive community impact through outreach programs and sustainable practices that support long-term growth and wellbeing.

Our Goals
Establish a modern, all-in-one facility offering premium car wash, laundry, and sneaker cleaning with a dedicated customisation corner.
Foster strong community engagement by building meaningful partnerships and supporting local initiatives and educational programs.
Adopt sustainable practices throughout all operations, promoting environmentally responsible methods for a cleaner, greener future.
Expand Elpaso's footprint across Rustenburg and North West, bringing quality cleaning services to every community we serve.
